# Data and AI Disclosure

**Data source:** Synthetic teaching dataset generated for this course package.

**Time period:** Monthly data from 2019 to 2025.

**Unit of analysis:** One row represents one month, one region, and one transport mode.

**What the data includes:** Date, region, mode, trips, coordinates, methodology note, and an index relative to a 2019 baseline.

**What the data excludes:** Real service disruptions, real provider metadata, real operational constraints, demographic variables, and real patronage counts.

**Cleaning and transformations:** Dates are parsed, rows with invalid dates are dropped, row counts are validated, filters are applied, and latest-month rankings are calculated.

**Primary visual task:** Show change over time and compare recovery by mode.

**FT Visual Vocabulary category:** Change over time, ranking, distribution, spatial.

**Known limitations:** The data is synthetic and should not be used for real policy conclusions.

**AI assistance used:** Example only; replace with project-specific information.

**Human verification performed:** Example only; at minimum, verify row counts, data source, chart mappings, and AI-generated code.

**Appropriate use:** Learning app structure, interaction, caching, visual vocabulary, and disclosure.

**Inappropriate use:** Drawing real conclusions about public transport recovery.
