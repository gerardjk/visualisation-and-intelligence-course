# Visual Vocabulary sorting cards

Print and cut the question cards and task cards. Give each group the question cards first. Distribute the task cards only after groups have described the comparison required by each question.

## Question cards

---

**Which service is furthest below its target?**

---

**Do neighbourhoods with more tree cover record lower summer temperatures?**

---

**Which transport modes carry the most passengers?**

---

**How are household incomes distributed?**

---

**How has rental affordability changed since 2015?**

---

**How large is each university’s research income?**

---

**How is the annual budget divided among programs?**

---

**Where are emergency response times longest?**

---

**How do passengers transfer between transport modes?**

---

**Which groups recovered fastest, and which remain below baseline?**

---

**Are waiting times becoming less predictable?**

---

**Where does material leave the production process?**

---

## Task cards

---

**Deviation** — compare values with a baseline, target or reference

---

**Correlation** — examine whether variables move together

---

**Ranking** — determine order

---

**Distribution** — examine spread, clusters, gaps and outliers

---

**Change over time** — examine temporal pattern

---

**Magnitude** — compare size

---

**Part-to-whole** — examine composition

---

**Spatial** — examine location and geographic pattern

---

**Flow** — trace movement, transfer or connection

---

## Debrief prompts

- Which questions fit more than one category?
- What word in the question signals the comparison?
- Which classification changes when the audience changes?
- Which chart suggestions appeared before the task was clear?
