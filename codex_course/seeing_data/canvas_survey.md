## Question

**For the later group assignment, which working environment would you prefer?**

- A group where approved AI-assisted recording or transcription tools may be used
- A group where AI-assisted recording or transcription tools will not be used
- No preference

## Description and safeguards

The survey states that:

- responses are used to support compatible group formation;
- there is no academic advantage or penalty;
- students do not need to explain their choice;
- an AI-compatible group preference is not blanket consent to every recording;
- groups must still agree on the tool, purpose, access, retention and deletion arrangements;
- students may contact the subject coordinator privately if their preference changes.
