# Microsoft Forms specification — group recording preference

## Purpose

Use this form to support compatible group formation for the later group assignment. It is a preference survey, not blanket consent to being recorded.

## Recommended Microsoft Forms settings

- Restrict responses to people in the UTS organisation.
- Record the respondent’s UTS identity so the preference can be used for group formation.
- Do not display response summaries to students.
- Allow respondents to edit or replace their response until groups are finalised.
- Limit access to the subject coordinator and staff directly involved in forming groups.
- Set and communicate a response deadline.
- Delete or securely dispose of the preference data when it is no longer required.

## Form title

**Group working preference — AI-assisted recording and transcription**

## Form introduction

For the later group assignment, some groups may choose to use an approved AI-assisted recording or transcription tool during meetings. Other groups will work without those tools.

Your response will be used only to help form groups with compatible preferences. There is no academic advantage or penalty attached to any response. You do not need to explain your choice, and you may change it before groups are finalised.

Selecting an AI-recording-compatible group is not blanket consent to every recording. Before recording begins, all group members must agree on the specific tool, purpose, people with access, retention period and deletion arrangements.

Do not place confidential, sensitive, personal or restricted information into an external AI tool.

## Required question

**For the later group assignment, which working environment would you prefer?**

Type: Choice, single answer, required.

- A group where approved AI-assisted recording or transcription tools may be used
- A group where AI-assisted recording or transcription tools will not be used
- No preference

## Optional question

**Would you like the subject coordinator to contact you privately before groups are formed?**

Type: Choice, single answer, optional.

- Yes
- No

Do not ask students to disclose disability, health, cultural, religious, privacy or other personal reasons for their preference.

## Suggested closing message

Thank you. Your preference will be considered when groups are formed. Group membership remains subject to enrolment numbers and workable group sizes. Contact the subject coordinator privately if your preference changes or you have concerns about the grouping process.
