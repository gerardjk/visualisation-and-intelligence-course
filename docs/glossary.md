# Glossary

**AI Provenance Matrix**: A record of significant intelligent-tool interactions, outputs, decisions, and verification steps.

**Data and AI Disclosure**: A standard explanation of data source, transformations, limitations, intelligent-tool use, and appropriate/inappropriate use.

**FT Visual Vocabulary**: A chart-selection scaffold that groups visual forms by communication task.

**Public data product**: A deployed interactive artefact that another person can access, understand, trust, and use.

**Specialised visualisation**: A visual form such as a map, flow diagram, network, animation, or 3D visualisation that is justified only when it serves the task.

**User story**: A statement of the form “As a [user], I want to [task], so that I can [purpose].”

**Visual task**: The analytical purpose of a visualisation, such as ranking, distribution, change over time, or spatial pattern.
