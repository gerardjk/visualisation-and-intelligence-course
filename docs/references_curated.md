# Curated References

This is a selective reading list. The broader research brief can inform instructor preparation, but students should not be assigned hundreds of readings.

## Core visualisation

- Cairo, A. *How Charts Lie.*
- Munzner, T. “A Nested Model for Visualization Design and Validation.”
- Franconeri, S.L. et al. “The Science of Visual Data Communication: What Works.”
- Wickham, H. “Tidy Data.”
- Financial Times Visual Vocabulary.

## Dashboard design

- Sarikaya, A. et al. “What Do We Talk About When We Talk About Dashboards?”
- Bach, B. et al. “Dashboard Design Patterns.”
- Setlur, V. et al. “Heuristics for Supporting Cooperative Dashboard Design.”
- Wexler, S., Shaffer, J. and Cotgreave, A. *The Big Book of Dashboards.*

## Narrative, uncertainty, accessibility

- Segel, E. and Heer, J. “Narrative Visualization.”
- Hullman, J. “Why Authors Don’t Visualize Uncertainty.”
- Hullman, J. and Diakopoulos, N. “Visualization Rhetoric.”
- Kay, M. et al. “When (ish) is My Bus?”
- Correll, M. and Gleicher, M. “Error Bars Considered Harmful.”
- Lundgard, A. and Satyanarayan, A. “Accessible Visualization via Natural Language Descriptions.”

## Ethics, data governance, and critique

- D’Ignazio, C. and Klein, L. *Data Feminism.*
- Maiam nayri Wingara and AIGI. *Indigenous Data Sovereignty Communique.*
- Carroll, S.R. et al. “The CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance.”
- Correll, M. “Ethical Dimensions of Visualization Research.”

## Intelligent tools and provenance

- Long, D. and Magerko, B. “What is AI Literacy?”
- Wang, H.W. et al. “DracoGPT.”
- Liu, X. et al. “Are LLMs Capable of Data-Based Statistical and Causal Reasoning?”
- Gebru, T. et al. “Datasheets for Datasets.”
- Mitchell, M. et al. “Model Cards for Model Reporting.”
- Holland, S. et al. “The Dataset Nutrition Label.”

## Specialised visualisation and web visualisation

- Robertson, G. et al. “Effectiveness of Animation in Trend Visualization.”
- Tversky, B. et al. “Animation: Can it Facilitate?”
- Marriott, K. et al. “Immersive Analytics: Time to Reconsider the Value of 3D.”
- Yang, Y. et al. “Tilt Map.”
- Monmonier, M. *How to Lie with Maps.*

## Assessment and pedagogy

- TEQSA. *Assessment Reform for the Age of Artificial Intelligence.*
- Bearman, M. et al. “Developing Evaluative Judgement for a Time of Generative AI.”
- Dawson, P. et al. “Validity Matters More Than Cheating.”
- Corbin, T., Dawson, P. and Liu, D. “Talk Is Cheap.”
- Sotiriadou, P. et al. “The Role of Authentic Assessment to Preserve Academic Integrity and Promote Skill Development and Employability.”
- Sentance, S., Waite, J. and Kallia, M. PRIMM.
