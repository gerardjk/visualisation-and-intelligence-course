# Dataset Guidance

The starter app uses a synthetic public-transport recovery dataset. It is designed for teaching only.

## Recommended real dataset categories

- Transport for NSW Open Data.
- ABS Labour Force.
- ABS Census.
- RBA statistical tables.
- AEMO NEM data.
- City of Melbourne pedestrian counters.
- AEC aggregate election or referendum results.
- BOM climate data.

## Data-trap prompts

Students should check:

```text
Definitions stable?
Collection method stable?
Methodology changes?
Boundary changes?
Missing values?
Confidentialisation or perturbation?
Time-zone or daylight-saving issues?
Public-data safety?
```
