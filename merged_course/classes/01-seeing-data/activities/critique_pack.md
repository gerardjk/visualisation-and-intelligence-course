# Seeing Data — critique pack

## Artefact

Use `assets/program_satisfaction_truncated.png`.

The chart compares satisfaction after two delivery formats:

- Standard format: 82%
- Redesigned format: 86%

The vertical axis begins at 80%, and the title reads “Redesign dramatically improves satisfaction.” The values are genuine measurements, but no sampling method, group sizes, uncertainty or assignment process is supplied.

## Generated interpretation

> The redesigned format caused a dramatic improvement in student satisfaction. The four-point increase proves that the new approach is substantially more effective and should replace the standard format across all subjects.

## Student task

Annotate each sentence or clause as:

- supported directly;
- plausible but unverified;
- unsupported;
- contradicted by the available evidence.

Then complete the Critique and Repair activity sheet.

## Verification questions

- Were participants randomly assigned?
- How many responses were collected in each group?
- Are the groups comparable?
- What were the confidence intervals?
- Was the same survey instrument used?
- Is a four-percentage-point difference practically important?
- Does the result generalise to other subjects?

## Repair target

Use `assets/program_satisfaction_repaired.png` only after students have proposed their own repair.

Suggested caption:

> Reported satisfaction was 86% for the redesigned format and 82% for the standard format, a four-percentage-point difference. Without sample sizes, uncertainty or information about group assignment, the comparison does not establish that the redesign caused the difference.
