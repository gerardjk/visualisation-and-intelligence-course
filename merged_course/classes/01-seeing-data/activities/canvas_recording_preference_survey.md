# Canvas survey: group recording preference

## Purpose

Use the published Canvas survey to support compatible group formation for the
later group assignment. It records a working preference; it is not blanket
consent to being recorded.

## Live survey

- **Title:** Group working preference. AI-assisted recording
- **Location:** Canvas → Get started
- **Link:** <https://canvas.uts.edu.au/courses/41964/quizzes/140096>
- **Type:** ungraded survey
- **Attempts:** one
- **Student result visibility:** hidden
- **Staff access:** authorised teaching staff can view identified responses for
  group allocation

Students choose one of three positions: an AI-recording-compatible group, a
group that will not use AI-assisted recording or transcription, or no
preference. Neither choice carries an academic advantage or penalty.

## Privacy and consent script

Tell students:

> Your response is visible to authorised teaching staff, not to other
> students, and is used only to support group allocation. Choosing an
> AI-recording-compatible group is not consent to every recording. Before any
> recording begins, all group members must agree on the tool, purpose, access,
> retention and deletion arrangements.

Do not ask students to disclose disability, health, cultural, religious,
privacy or other personal reasons. A student who wants to change a submitted
preference before groups are finalised should contact the subject coordinator
privately; the survey itself permits one attempt.

## Operational checklist

- Keep response summaries hidden from students.
- Restrict response access to staff forming groups.
- Announce a response deadline before group formation.
- Provide a private contact route for changes or concerns.
- Delete or securely dispose of preference data when it is no longer required.
- Confirm any proposed recording tool against current UTS privacy, records,
  accessibility, assessment and AI requirements.
