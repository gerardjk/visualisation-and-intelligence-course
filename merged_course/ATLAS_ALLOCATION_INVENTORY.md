# Atlas allocation inventory

Snapshot: 12 August 2026 (Australia/Sydney).

## Result

The Atlas has **103 numbered visualisations**. The separate AIATSIS prelude is
not included in this count because it is not one of the numbered entries and
the course coverage rules explicitly exclude it.

- **69 of 103** have a concrete destination in the canonical local course plan.
- **34 of 103** are currently unallocated.
- **23 unique entries are represented in released Canvas material now:** 16
  Atlas-derived numbered figures in the published Seeing Data slides and 7
  clear Atlas analogues in released A1.
- The Seeing Data workbook repeats Anscombe rather than adding a seventeenth
  released entry.
- The remaining allocations are in prepared but unreleased lesson material.

Counts are for unique Atlas entries, not placements. Reuse is deliberate:
Anscombe appears in slides and the workbook; climate stripes appears in both
lesson decks; several activity-card examples also have an A1 analogue.

## Sources and counting rules

The inventory was traced from:

- `quarto-book/atlas.qmd` for the numbered Atlas catalogue;
- `classes/01-seeing-data/beamer/seeing-data.tex` for Lesson 1 slide embeds;
- `classes/01-seeing-data/notebooks/seeing_data_lab.ipynb` for the released
  workbook;
- `classes/02-visual-forms/beamer/choosing-visual-forms.tex` for the canonical
  Lesson 2 slide allocation;
- `scripts/build_figure_cards.py` for both activity-card packs;
- `scripts/build_a1_pool.py`, `artefact_pool.md`, and the released A1 archive
  for assessment analogues;
- the Canvas API for module publication state and the exact stored Week 3 PDF.

“Allocated” means one of three things, kept distinct above: an actual Atlas
reproduction embedded in lesson material; an explicit workbook recreation of
the same named dataset; or a clearly corresponding published A1 artefact. Mere
similarity of topic or chart family is not counted.

The companion `ATLAS_ALLOCATION_INVENTORY.csv` gives one row per Atlas entry
and exposes both the local and Canvas Week 3 columns so the deck drift can be
filtered directly.
