# 8-Week Schedule

| Week | Lecture | In-class studio | Due / checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seeing Data: Visualisation, Intelligence, and Interpretation | Critique human-made and generated charts | Diagnostic quiz; Critique 1 draft |
| 2 | Choosing Visual Forms: Encoding, Tidy Data, and the Visual Vocabulary | Visual Vocabulary sorting and redesign | Quiz 2; Critique 2 draft |
| 3 | Dashboards, Personas, and User Stories: Tableau Public | Tableau dashboard build and persona defence | Quiz 3; A3 dashboard submission |
| 4 | Interactive Data Apps with Streamlit | Build local Streamlit MVP | Quiz 4; group project plan |
| 5 | From Prototype to Public Data Product | Deploy app, add disclosure, test user stories | Quiz 5; A4 public data product checkpoint |
| 6 | Web Visualisation Studio: Maps, Flows, Animation, 3D, and JavaScript Literacy | Specialised visualisation decision and optional extension | Quiz 6; specialised decision draft |
| 7 | Responsible Interpretation and Visualisation Quality Review | Review another app and revise own app | Quiz 7; A6 quality review; Critique 3 |
| 8 | Present and Defend: Visual Intelligence in Practice | Group presentation and individual oral defence | A5 final app; A7 oral defence |

## Suggested weekly preparation rhythm

- 1 required reading or resource.
- 1 short technical preparation task where needed.
- 1 auto-graded quiz.
- 1 studio artefact or project milestone.

## Key timing notes

- Students should form groups by the end of Week 3.
- Groups should have a local Streamlit prototype by the end of Week 4.
- Groups should have a deployed app by the end of Week 5.
- Week 6 should not become a JavaScript assignment; it is literacy plus optional extension.
- Week 7 is the main interpretive quality review week.
