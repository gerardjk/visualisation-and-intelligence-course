# A6 — Visualisation Quality Review

## Weight

10%

## Type

Group, peer-generated and staff-marked.

## Description

Each group reviews another group’s app using a structured report. The goal is to identify how the app could mislead, break, confuse, or fail to disclose important limitations.

## Required review categories

```text
1. Main claim and audience fit.
2. User stories and app features.
3. Visual Vocabulary category and chart fit.
4. Visual interpretation risk.
5. Input/filter robustness.
6. Data loading or reliability issue.
7. Data and AI Disclosure.
8. Accessibility/usability.
9. AI provenance.
10. Specialised visualisation decision.
11. Highest-priority improvement.
```

If the app includes live intelligent-tool output, also review AI-output risk.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Specificity of observations | 30% |
| Evidence or reproduction steps | 20% |
| Correct prioritisation | 20% |
| Constructive recommendations | 20% |
| Professional tone | 10% |
